2022 – 2023 Fastest rate hike cycle in 40 years

The Federal Reserve raised rates from near zero to over 5% in eighteen months to fight the sharpest inflation since the early 1980s. Short-dated yields rose far faster than long-dated ones, driving the curve deeply inverted — historically a reliable recession signal.

Reading this curve US curve is inverted

The 2Y yields 4.22% while the 10Y yields 3.51% — a spread of −71bp. Investors are accepting less return to lock money up for longer, which happens when they expect rates, growth, or both to fall.
